Seasons Runner Game

How to Play

How to Jump

Press the Spacebar, click with your mouse, or tap the screen to make the cat jump over obstacles. You can also double jump for extra height by jumping again while in the air!

How the Game Works

You control the cat through endless runner levels. You must jump over or avoid obstacles. You progress through the seasons in this order: Autumn → Winter → Spring → Summer.

Each season has 3 levels. Only the first available season is unlocked initially. Completing all 3 levels of a season unlocks the next season. After completing a season, you return to the homepage.

Your Goal

Progress through all four seasons and reach the Finish Line celebration after completing Summer!
